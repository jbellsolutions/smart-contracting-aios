# SOP writer

Jesse, Oct 28: "I'd love to create… standard operating procedures from in the office to the guys on site… so there's an accountability."

## Inputs
- Screenshots, recordings, notes or Jesse's explanation
- `vault/sops/` (existing SOPs)
- For Ascora and Xero steps: the vendor's own help pages, read live, cited by URL

## Steps
- [ ] Ask who does it, when, and what "done" looks like, if the material doesn't say.
- [ ] Write the SOP: **Who · When · Steps** (numbered, one click or action each, with the screen name) **· Check it's done · If you get stuck** (the most likely wrong turn and the way back).
- [ ] Site SOPs fit on one phone screen; office SOPs can be longer.
- [ ] "How do I get back?" questions: find the step that was missed, give the way back from the vendor's help pages, and offer to add it to the SOP. In Xero, give Mike or Jesse the steps; never make the correction yourself.
- [ ] Save as a draft in `queue/drafts/sops/`; it moves to `vault/sops/office/` or `vault/sops/site/` on Jesse's yes.

## Output
One SOP per file, plain words, NZ English.

## Done means
Someone who has never done the task could follow it, and Jesse has approved it.

## Never
- Invent a menu, button or setting. If you can't confirm a step, mark it "check this step" for Jesse.
- Change anything in Ascora or Xero.
