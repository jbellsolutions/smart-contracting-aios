# Approvals

Three tiers. When in doubt, it's the higher tier.

| Tier | What | Who says yes |
|---|---|---|
| 0: free | Reading, drafting, summarising, writing the vault, the Needs-Jesse list, run logs; messages and reports to Jesse or Mike themselves (the brief, the money snapshot, drafts for their yes) | Nobody: just do it and log it |
| 1: Jesse's yes, each time | Anything a customer, partner, prospect, crew member or the public will see: emails, texts, posts, calendar invites to others; Facebook replies (Jesse posts those himself); changes in Ascora or GoHighLevel other than prices | Jesse, in his own words, about the exact item shown |
| Mike's sign-off | Every price (a quote's rates and totals) and anything about the books | Mike. The agent drafts; Mike decides; Mike or Jesse enters it |
| 2: never by an agent | Moving money; paying a bill or a partner; sending or voiding an invoice; changing anything in Xero; changing a price in Ascora; deleting their data; changing a password or a login | Mike or Jesse does it themselves. The agent prepares it and says so |

## How to ask (the plain-English rule)

On Oct 28 Jesse had to ask what a prompt was for. So every approval request:
1. says **what will happen** ("I'll post these three before/after photos to Instagram and Facebook"),
2. says **who will see it**,
3. shows **the exact text or image** that will go,
4. ends with **the exact words to reply** ("Reply **post it** to post, or tell me what to change").

A bare "yes" approves nothing new. One item, one thread, one keyword.

## Typed codes (the guarded actions)

A change to the Revenue Partner's computer, a question to it, turning its sending back on, or lifting `state/STOP` here: the tool shows the exact action and an 8-character code in a window on Jesse's screen (never in the chat), and only Jesse typing exactly `yes <code>` approves it (`docs/CONVENTIONS.md`, "Jesse's typed approval codes"). The code is for that one action, used once. This file itself is changed only by Jesse or by Justin's team; the chat can't edit it.

## Sending from this PC

This workspace drafts; it doesn't send. A hook allows connector tools only to read or draft, so a standing approval alone can't make anything send. Jesse sends from his own apps. For a job that should send by itself (customer texts on rain days, pre-start reminders), Justin's team opens that one send path, and Jesse's standing approval covers that exact message.

## Standing approvals

A standing approval covers one exact template or action, in Jesse's words, recorded by the Chief of Staff in `context/standing-approvals.md` with the date. Jesse said on Oct 28 that site posts may need no check once "it's the same every time": that becomes a standing approval only when he says so for a named post type, after a run of approved posts. None exist at install.
