# NZ rules for every email, text and post

Plain summary for the agents, not legal advice. Jesse's lawyer has the last word. The partner program's fuller notes are in `modules/partner-program/program/compliance-nz.md`.

- **Unsolicited Electronic Messages Act 2007** (emails and texts): a commercial message needs consent (express, inferred from a published business address and a relevant offer, or deemed), must say who it's from and how to reach them, and must have a working way to unsubscribe that's honoured. Anyone who says no is never contacted again.
- **Privacy Act 2020**: collect only what the job needs, say why, keep it safe, let people see or correct what we hold about them. Customer details stay in Ascora, Xero and GoHighLevel; the vault holds summaries.
- **Fair Trading Act 1986**: no misleading claims. No invented reviews, results or "guarantees". Prices only from Mike's sheets with his sign-off.
- **Photos of customers' properties**: no house numbers, number plates or faces without the customer's OK. When unsure, crop or blur.
